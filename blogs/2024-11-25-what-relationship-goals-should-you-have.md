Photo by Alesia Kazantceva on Unsplash Once the relationship between two people morphs into something more serious than a casual hookup or some form of dating online, such as a textationship , it’s time to think about shared relationship goals. What are relationship goals? Relationship goals are exactly what they sound like — goals for couples that they want to achieve to improve or progress in their relationship.
